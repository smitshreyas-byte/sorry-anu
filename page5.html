My Apology

Dear Anu ❤️

I don't know if words are enough to express how sorry I am.

The last few days with you were truly special. Those moments, those smiles, those conversations... I will always keep them close to my heart.

But I also understand that my actions hurt you. Sending those reels was a mistake. I should have thought about your feelings and protected your trust.

I know you were hurt because you care about us. And seeing you cry because of something I did is something I regret deeply.

I am not writing this to force you to forget. I know trust takes time. I only want to show you that I understand my mistake.

I promise to be more thoughtful. I promise to respect your feelings. I promise to value the beautiful relationship we have.

Thank you for every smile. Thank you for every memory. Thank you for being a beautiful part of my life.

I am truly sorry, Anu. ❤️
Shreyash
